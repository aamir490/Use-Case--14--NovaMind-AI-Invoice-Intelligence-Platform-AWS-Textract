## What Your Project Is

**Serverless Invoice Processing & Anomaly Detection Pipeline**

You built an automated AWS pipeline that takes a raw invoice image (photo/scan), 
 runs it through OCR, and then uses a Large Language Model to act as a smart auditor — flagging anything that looks 
  wrong or suspicious, without any human manually reading the invoice.

---

## What That DynamoDB Output Tells You

That `llm_analysis` field is the AI output from **Amazon Bedrock (Nova Micro)**. Your pipeline successfully:

1. Received `invoice_4.jpg` from S3
2. Fed it through **Amazon Textract** → extracted all the text, line items, prices, tax rates
3. Sent that raw text to **Bedrock** with the prompt: *"check for inconsistencies and unusual charges"*
4. Stored the AI's findings back in **DynamoDB** alongside the structured data

The LLM found **real problems** in that invoice:

| Finding | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Date `"23 Jan - 2025"` | Non-standard format — could indicate a manually typed/fake invoice |
| Invoice number `"IN-15"` | Looks informal — real invoices use formats like `INV-0015` |
| Units `"1 NOS"`, `"1 KGS"` vs `"1 KG"` | Inconsistent — suggests the invoice wasn't generated by proper billing software |
| Subtotal ₹900 + tax ≠ line items | The individual item prices don't actually add up to ₹900 — **this is a real red flag** |
| IGST rates 0%, 3%, 5%, 12% on same invoice | Unusual mix — needs verification |
| `"Walnuts 5% Tax Item"` as a product name | Tax rate embedded in item name — non-standard, possibly manual entry |
| Orange Powder ₹448/kg, Cheese ₹112/kg | Flagged as potentially high — model is cross-checking against general price expectations |

## The Core Value of Your Project

Your pipeline essentially does **automated invoice fraud/error detection**. In a real business context this would catch:
- Manually doctored invoices
- Billing software bugs producing wrong totals
- Vendors charging wrong tax rates
- Data entry errors before payment is made

All of this runs **serverlessly** — you upload a photo, and in seconds you have structured data + an audit report in DynamoDB, with zero human in the loop.
